# Data sources for EMODnet Human Activities

The corresponding standards and reusable OGC Blocks are specified in the [source-to-block mapping](emodnet_human_activities_bblock_mapping.md).

| Demonstrator case | Dataset description | Anticipated file type | Added value for EMODnet (Human Activities) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All | Unified dataset showing development of wind parks in the three areas (collected from stakeholders, development plans, or other) | GEOJSON or similar | The current dataset of wind parks on EMODnet dates from 2014 and is missing newly developed or under development areas such as the three SEADOTs cases. |
| Norwegian North Sea | Miljødirektoratet. (2026). Naturbase – Verneområder (Protected areas, incl. marine MPAs) [Data set]. Norwegian Environment Agency. Accessed [DATE] at https://kartkatalog.miljodirektoratet.no/dataset/Details/0 (download via karteksport.miljodirektoratet.no). Licence: NLOD. | GEOJSON or similar | Higher quality data on MPAs and cables than is available on EMODnet currently can be accessed through the Norwegian Environmental Agency. |
| Norwegian North Sea | Fiskeridirektoratet. (2026). Fangstdata (seddel) koblet med fartøydata – landings linked with vessel data, by landing municipality and species [Open data set]. Norwegian Directorate of Fisheries. Filter by Utsira (kommune 1151). Accessed [DATE] at https://www.fiskeridir.no/statistikk-tall-og-analyse/data-og-statistikk-om-yrkesfiske/apne-data-fangstdata-seddel-koblet-med-fartoydata. GIS layers via https://open-data-fiskeridirektoratet-fiskeridir.hub.arcgis.com/. Vessel register API: https://www.fiskeridir.no/Yrkesfiske/Registre-og-skjema/Fartoeyregisteret/api. Licence: NLOD.<br><br>ICES. (2024). VMS and logbook spatial fishing-effort products (WGSFD outputs, North Sea / North-East Atlantic, 2009–2024) [Data set]. International Council for the Exploration of the Sea, Working Group on Spatial Fisheries Data. https://www.ices.dk/community/groups/pages/wgsfd.aspx (NB: Norway is currently a non-submitting country to this data call – for Utsira-specific effort, supplement with Fiskeridirektoratet VMS/landings). | .csv | More high quality data on fisheries landings is available from the Norwegian Fishing Directorate and ICES. |
| Norwegian North Sea | Norwegian Coastal Administration (Kystverket) / BarentsWatch. (2026). Historical AIS vessel-traffic data (Norwegian EEZ) [Data set]. Open historical AIS via https://kystdatahuset.no (search/download, no auth) and live API/map services via https://nais.kystverket.no and BarentsWatch (https://www.barentswatch.no). Used to derive vessel density / shipping activity around Utsira and Utsira Nord. Licence: NLOD. | GEOJSON or similar | Higher resolution data are available from local sources than is currently available through EMODnet. |
| Norwegian North Sea | Norwegian Mapping Authority (Kartverket) / Geonorge. (2026). Marine basemap, bathymetry and seabed cable/pipeline routes [Data set]. Available via https://www.geonorge.no and https://norgeskart.no; download/WMS/WFS through Kartverket APIs (https://www.kartverket.no/en/api-and-data). Used as base layers and for verifying submarine cable / pipeline corridors near Utsira Nord. Licence: NLOD. | GEOJSON or similar | Higher resolution data are available from local sources than is currently available through EMODnet. |
| German Bight | Offshore windfarm monopile locations (from stakeholders) | GEOJSON or similar | No similar dataset is currently available on EMODnet. |
| German Bight | Vessel density maps (AIS-based, monthly resolution) | GEOJSON or similar | Higher resolution data are available from local sources than is currently available through EMODnet. |
| German Bight | Port traffic (Helgoland, Cuxhaven, etc.) | GEOJSON or similar | Higher resolution data are available from local sources than is currently available through EMODnet. |
| German Bight | Fisheries intensity | .csv | Higher resolution data are available from local sources than is currently available through EMODnet. |
| Central Baltic (Gotland) | Swedish logbook data from all licensed fishermen were collected between 1999 and 2024 on 24 November 2025 with permission from the Swedish Maritime and Water Management Authority (SwAM). | .csv | No similar dataset is currently available on EMODnet. |
| Central Baltic (Gotland) | European Commission. (2026). [Data set]. The Community Fishing Fleet Register [Data set]. European Commission, Directorate-General for Maritime Affairs and Fisheries. http://data.europa.eu/88u/dataset/the-community-fishing-fleet-register (Original work published 2014). | .csv | The dataset has been quality assured through manual verification of fishing vessels, filtering out non-fishing vessels in the new dataset. |
| Central Baltic (Gotland) | Global Fishing Watch. (2025). Public global fishing effort v4.0 [Data set, updated daily]. Data set accessed 2026-03-21 at https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-apis/. | .csv | Higher resolution data are available from local sources than is currently available through EMODnet. |
